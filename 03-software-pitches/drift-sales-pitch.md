## Why it made the cut
It's the second canonical example of the "strategic narrative" pitch. It shows the Zuora structure working in a very different category. Drift used this story to help create the "conversational marketing" category.

## The narrative
| Step | Drift's version |
|---|---|
| **Change in the world** | Buyers are now in control. They expect the instant, messaging-style experience they get as consumers. |
| **Winners & losers** | Companies still using the old playbook (gated forms → wait for a follow-up email → "book a demo in 3 days") lose buyers to companies that respond in real time. |
| **Promised Land** | Talk to buyers the moment they're interested: *no forms, no waiting.* |
| **Magic gifts** | Chatbots, real-time routing to reps, meeting booking inside the chat. |
| **Evidence** | Drift used its own funnel as proof: it got rid of its own lead forms and showed the results. |

## Key line
> The old way of buying (fill out a form, wait for a follow-up) was built for the seller's convenience, not the buyer's.

This line works because it **gives the problem a name the buyer has personally lived through** as a consumer.

## Lesson
The most powerful evidence is **"we did this to ourselves first."** Using your own product on your own business makes a very credible proof point.
