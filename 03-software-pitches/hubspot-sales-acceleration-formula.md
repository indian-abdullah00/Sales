## Why it made the cut
An engineer's approach to building a software sales team: **hiring, training, management and demand generation each treated as a measurable, repeatable process.** It's one of the few books by someone who built a SaaS sales org from the first hire to hundreds of reps.

## Core ideas
- **Hiring formula:** score candidates on the traits that predict success in *your* org. At HubSpot, **coachability** was the top predictor, along with curiosity, prior success, intelligence and work ethic.
- **Training formula:** reps learn the *buyer's job* first (HubSpot reps had to build their own blogs and get certified in inbound marketing).
- **Management formula:** coach **one skill at a time**, chosen from the metrics.
- **Demand generation formula:** inbound marketing feeds the funnel. Sales and marketing share a **service-level agreement (SLA)**.
- **Buyer-centric selling:** sell the way the buyer wants to buy; help first.
