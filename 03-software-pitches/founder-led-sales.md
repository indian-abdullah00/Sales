## Why it made the cut
Most sales material assumes you already have a sales team, a brand and customer logos. These sources cover the hardest case: **a founder selling an unknown product with no proof.** That's how Stripe, Airbnb and most of today's software giants got their first customers.

## 1. The "Collison Installation" (Stripe)
From Paul Graham's *Do Things That Don't Scale*: when someone agreed to try Stripe, the Collison brothers didn't say "great, I'll send you a link." They said:

> **"Right then, give me your laptop."**

...and set the person up on the spot.

**Lesson:** shrink the gap between "yes" and actually using the product to zero. The moment of agreement is when intent is highest; every delay after it loses deals.

## 2. Paul Graham on early sales
- **Recruit users by hand.** Founders resist it because it's tedious and they fear rejection, but it's what works.
- **Delight the first users** far beyond what would scale.
- Companies grow because founders *make* them grow, not because "if you build it, they will come."

## 3. *Founding Sales* (Pete Kazanjy): the practical manual
A free, chapter-by-chapter guide for technical founders:
- **Sales narrative:** problem → impact → solution → why us.
- **Prospecting lists:** target account and persona definitions.
- **Discovery:** qualify hard. "No" is the second-best answer.
- **Demo:** show the 'aha' fast, tailored to the pains you uncovered.
- **Closing and pipeline:** stage definitions with exit criteria.

## Key dialogue: founder cold outreach *(illustrative, in Founding Sales style)*
> "Hi Priya, I'm the founder of [X]. We built it after watching our own support team lose roughly a day a week tagging tickets by hand. I'm not trying to sell you anything yet. I'm talking to 20 support leaders to learn how they handle this today. Would you be open to 20 minutes? Happy to share what I'm hearing from the others."

**Why it works:** founders get meetings that reps can't, by asking for *advice* and offering what they've learned from other buyers. Once there's a fit, the advice call can turn into a sale.
