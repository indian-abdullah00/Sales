## Why it made the cut
Dunford updates the "strategic narrative" for **today's informed buyers, who have already seen the Zuora-style pitch many times.** She builds the pitch from positioning, especially *competitive alternatives* and *differentiated value*, so it holds up when the buyer is comparing options.

## Positioning (from *Obviously Awesome*): the five components
1. **Competitive alternatives**: what customers would do if you didn't exist (often spreadsheets, an intern, or doing nothing).
2. **Unique attributes**: what you have that the alternatives don't.
3. **Value (and proof)**: what those attributes make possible for customers.
4. **Target customer characteristics**: who cares most about that value.
5. **Market category**: the context that makes your value obvious.

## The Sales Pitch structure (from *Sales Pitch*)
| # | Step | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Insight** (the setup) | How the market or buyer situation has changed. A teaching moment, as in Challenger. |
| 2 | **Alternatives** | The ways buyers try to solve this today, and where each falls short. *Be fair; buyers trust balance.* |
| 3 | **"Perfect world"** | What a great solution would have to do, given the insight. |
| 4 | **Introduce the product** | Show that you match the perfect world: **differentiated value** themes, 2–3 of them. |
| 5 | **Proof** | Demos, customer stories, data, *for each value theme*. |
| 6 | **Objection handling** | Raise the known concerns yourself and address them. |
| 7 | **Call to action** | A clear next step. |

## Key dialogue *(illustrative)*
> **Insight:** "Over the last two years, the average company went from 3 to 11 data sources feeding revenue reporting."
>
> **Alternatives:** "Most teams handle this in one of three ways: spreadsheets, which are flexible but break at scale; BI tools, which are powerful but need an analyst for every change; or the CRM's built-in reporting, which is easy but only sees CRM data."
>
> **Perfect world:** "Ideally you'd have something as flexible as a spreadsheet, that sees all 11 sources, and that a RevOps person can change without filing a ticket."
>
> **Product + value:** "That's what we do. Three things set us apart..."

## Lesson
**Name the alternatives, including your competitors, honestly.** Buyers will compare you with them anyway. If you set up the comparison, you control how it's framed.
