| **Author / Origin** | Neil Rackham |
| **Year** | 1988 |
| **Field** | Complex B2B sales |

## Why it made the cut
The best-researched discovery method there is. Almost every modern discovery approach (Gap, Sandler's pain funnel, MEDDPICC's "Identify Pain") reuses its logic.

## The four stages of a sales call
1. **Preliminaries**: brief rapport and purpose. *Don't overdo it.*
2. **Investigating**: SPIN questions (the core).
3. **Demonstrating capability**: present benefits tied **only** to explicit needs.
4. **Obtaining commitment**: aim for an *Advance*, not necessarily the order.

## The question sequence
| Stage | Purpose | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| **Situation** | Context | "How do you currently...?" |
| **Problem** | Surface implied needs | "Are you satisfied with...? What's difficult about...?" |
| **Implication** | Make the problem bigger and more urgent | "What effect does that have on...? What does that cost you?" |
| **Need-payoff** | Turn implied needs into explicit needs | "How would it help if...? Why is solving that important?" |

## Key dialogue: full illustrative call (data warehouse SaaS)
> **S:** "Who pulls together the weekly revenue report today?"
> **Buyer:** "Two analysts, from Salesforce, Stripe and our billing tool."
>
> **P:** "Do the numbers ever disagree between those systems?"
> **Buyer:** "Constantly. The CFO and sales leadership argue about which one is right."
>
> **I:** "When that happens, what happens to the board deck timeline?"
> **Buyer:** "It slips. Last quarter we sent it two days late."
>
> **I:** "And the analysts, how much of their week goes into reconciling instead of analysis?"
> **Buyer:** "Probably half."
>
> **N:** "If those numbers reconciled automatically, what would you have those analysts working on?"
> **Buyer:** "Churn modeling. We've wanted that for a year."
>
> *The buyer has now stated the value: board credibility plus a new churn capability. Only now do you show the product.*

## Watch-outs
- Don't run SPIN as a script; experienced buyers spot it. Let it flow as a conversation.
- Situation questions you could have answered with ten minutes on LinkedIn are wasted.
