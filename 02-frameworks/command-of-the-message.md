|---|---|
| **Type** | Value-messaging framework |
| **Author / Origin** | Force Management (John Kaplan et al.) |

| **Field** | Enterprise software |
| **Evidence tier** | **B: Proven at scale** |
| **Original** | [Force Management: Command of the Message](https://www.forcemanagement.com/command-of-the-message) |

## Why it made the cut
It's the messaging system behind a large number of enterprise SaaS sales orgs (often rolled out together with MEDDPICC). It answers the question every buyer is silently asking: **"Why should I do anything, why now, and why you?"**

## The Value Framework (one page per product)
| Component | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Before scenarios** | The buyer's current state and its problems |
| **Negative consequences** | What those problems cost |
| **After scenarios** | The future state |
| **Positive business outcomes** | Measurable results in that future |
| **Required capabilities** | What *any* solution must be able to do to get there |
| **Metrics** | How success is measured |
| **Differentiators** | Why you deliver the required capabilities better: *unique, defensible, and something the buyer actually needs* |
| **Proof points** | Customer stories and data |

## The Three Whys
1. **Why do anything?** (pain is bigger than the effort of change)
2. **Why now?** (cost of waiting, a trigger event)
3. **Why us?** (differentiators linked to required capabilities)

## Key dialogue: "Mantra" style positioning *(illustrative)*
> "Today your finance team spends the first ten days of every month reconciling billing, CRM and bank data by hand [Before]. That delays board reporting and hides revenue leakage, which runs to roughly 2% of ARR at companies your size [Negative consequence]. Teams that fix this close in three days and catch leakage automatically [After / PBO]. To get there, you need a system that reconciles all three sources automatically and flags exceptions [Required capability]. We're the only platform that does that natively with Stripe *and* NetSuite, without custom middleware [Differentiator]. One Series C fintech customer went from 9 days to 3 [Proof]."

> **Trap-setting question** (setting up a differentiator): "How important is it that the reconciliation works without your engineering team maintaining middleware?"

## Watch-outs
- It only works if reps actually internalize it. Read out as a script, it sounds like corporate marketing.
