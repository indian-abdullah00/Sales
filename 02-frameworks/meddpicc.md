# MEDDIC / MEDDPICC

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Deal qualification methodology |
| **Author / Origin** | Dick Dunkel, with Jack Napoli and John McMahon, at PTC (Parametric Technology) |
| **Year** | Mid-1990s |
| **Field** | Enterprise software |
| **Evidence tier** | **B: Proven at scale** |
| **Original** | [MEDDIC Academy](https://meddic.academy/); John McMahon, *The Qualified Sales Leader* (2021) |

## Why it made the cut
It's the qualification standard in enterprise software. It's credited with helping PTC grow very quickly in the 1990s (commonly cited as roughly $300M to $1B in a few years), and it's now used at Snowflake, MongoDB, Datadog and many other enterprise SaaS companies.

## The letters
| Letter | Meaning | Question to answer |
|---|---|---|
| **M** | **Metrics** | What measurable business result will they get? ($, %, time) |
| **E** | **Economic Buyer** | Who can say yes when everyone else says no, and have we met them? |
| **D** | **Decision Criteria** | What will they judge vendors on (technical, financial, other)? |
| **D** | **Decision Process** | What steps, people and timeline lead to a signed decision? |
| **P** | **Paper Process** | Legal, procurement, security review: how does a contract actually get signed? |
| **I** | **Identify Pain** | What business pain is bad enough to act on? |
| **C** | **Champion** | Who inside has power and influence, and will sell for you when you're not in the room? |
| **C** | **Competition** | Who and what else is in the running, *including doing nothing and building it themselves*? |

## Key dialogue: qualifying questions
- **Metrics:** "If this works, what number moves, and by how much? How would you measure it six months in?"
- **Economic Buyer:** "When a purchase this size has been approved before, whose signature was on it?"
- **Decision Criteria:** "If you had to rank the three things that will decide this, what are they?"
- **Decision Process:** "Walk me through what happens between 'we like this' and a signed contract. Who's involved at each step?"
- **Paper Process:** "Who in legal or procurement needs to see this, and how long did the last software contract take?"
- **Pain:** "What happens if you don't solve this this year?"
- **Champion test:** "Would you be willing to set up a meeting with the CFO and present the business case with me?" *(A real champion takes action; a "coach" only gives you information.)*
- **Competition:** "What alternatives are you looking at, including fixing this internally?"

## How to apply it
1. Use MEDDPICC as **deal review fields** in the CRM. Every gap is a risk.
2. **Test your champion.** Ask them to do something that costs them a little political capital.
3. Start the paper process early. Procurement is where quarter-end deals slip.

## Watch-outs
- It's a **qualification checklist, not a conversation**. Don't interrogate buyers letter by letter.
- It's overkill for small, transactional deals.
