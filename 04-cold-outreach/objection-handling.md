## Why it made the cut
It brings together the best-evidenced approaches to objections: data on *which* objections come up (Gong), *why* they arise (SPIN: most are created by pitching too early), and *how* to answer them (Voss, Sandler, JOLT).

## Universal principles
1. **Pause.** Top reps leave a beat before responding (Gong).
2. **Don't argue. Ask.** Turn the objection into a question.
3. **Acknowledge (label) before you redirect.** "It sounds like..." (Voss)
4. **Find the real objection.** The first one stated is often not the true one.
5. **Prevent rather than handle.** Better discovery means fewer objections (Rackham).

## The core framework: **Acknowledge → Clarify → Respond → Confirm**
> **Buyer:** "It's too expensive."
> **Acknowledge:** "That's fair. It's a real investment."
> **Clarify:** "When you say too expensive, is it compared with another option, with the budget you have, or with the value you're expecting?"
> **Respond (to the real issue):** "...Got it, compared with the budget. Earlier you said the manual process costs about $200k a year in analyst time. If the platform is $60k, does that change the picture, or is the issue more about which budget it comes from?"
> **Confirm:** "Does that address it, or is there something else making you hesitate?"

## Talk-track library
| Objection | Root cause | Talk track *(illustrative)* |
|---|---|---|
| **"Too expensive"** | Value not established | "Compared with what?" → bring it back to the cost of the problem. |
| **"We need to think about it"** | Indecision ([JOLT](../01-research-backed/jolt-effect-indecision.md)) | "Of course. Usually when people say that, it's either the price, the timing, or something I haven't explained well. Which is it for you?" |
| **"We're happy with our current vendor"** | Status quo | "Glad to hear it. What made you choose them? ... If you could improve one thing about them, what would it be?" |
| **"Not a priority right now"** | No urgency | "Makes sense. What *is* the priority this quarter? ... Is [problem] connected to that at all?" |
| **"Send me some info"** | Brush-off | "Happy to. What specifically would be useful, so I don't send you a pile of PDFs?" |
| **"We'll build it in-house"** | Underestimated cost | "That's often the right call. How many engineers, for how long? And who maintains it after launch?" |
| **"I need to talk to my boss"** | Not the economic buyer | "Of course. What do you think they'll ask? Would it help if I joined that conversation, or put together a one-page business case for you?" |
| **"Your competitor is cheaper"** | Price vs. value | "They may well be. If price were equal, who would you pick, and why?" |
