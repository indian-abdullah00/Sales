# Where to Keep Learning: Podcasts, Newsletters, Communities

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Curated list |
| **Field** | B2B software sales |
| **Evidence tier** | **C: Expert practitioner** |

## Why it made the cut
Books age; these sources publish **real calls, real emails and current data** every week. They also let you check the frameworks against what working sellers say actually works.

## Podcasts and video
| Source | Why it's worth your time | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **30 Minutes to President's Club** | Top-performing reps and leaders share *exact* talk tracks; regular live call breakdowns | [30mpc.com](https://www.30mpc.com/) |
| **Gong Labs / Gong blog** | Data-backed findings from millions of calls | [gong.io/resources/labs](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/) |
| **Josh Braun: Sales DNA / Badass B2B Growth** | Pressure-free outbound, many email and call rewrites | [joshbraun.com](https://www.joshbraun.com/) |
| **Sell Better / The Daily Sales Show** (formerly Sales Hacker community) | Practitioner sessions on cold calling, discovery, AI in sales | [captivate.fm show](https://sell-better-daily-sales-show.captivate.fm/) |
| **SaaStr** (Jason Lemkin) | SaaS go-to-market, founder-led sales, hiring your first AE | [saastr.com](https://www.saastr.com/) |
| **Lenny's Podcast / Newsletter** | Interviews on growth and GTM with top operators, including sales leaders | [lennysnewsletter.com](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/) |

## Communities
| Community | What it's good for |
|---|---|
| **Pavilion** (paid) | Sales leaders' peer network, playbooks, courses |
| **r/sales** (Reddit) | Unfiltered rep perspectives; good for checking hype against reality |
| **RevGenius** | Free GTM community, active Slack |
| **LinkedIn** | Many top practitioners (Braun, Farrokh, Cegelski, Jason Bay, Becc Holland, Kyle Coleman) post their scripts and teardowns there |

## How to use these well
1. Choose **one data source** (Gong) and **one practitioner source** (30MPC) and follow them consistently.
2. When something goes viral, check it against the [research tier](../01-research-backed/) before adopting it.
