## Why it made the cut
A multi-company study of **6,000+ sales reps** that sorted sellers into profiles and measured which profile performs best. It's the most cited modern counterpoint to "build relationships first."

## Core findings
Five rep profiles:
1. **The Hard Worker**: puts in the effort, makes more calls
2. **The Challenger**: teaches, tailors, takes control
3. **The Relationship Builder**: generous, likeable, avoids tension
4. **The Lone Wolf**: follows instincts, hard to manage
5. **The Reactive Problem Solver**: detail-oriented, reliable follow-up

Results:
- Among *high performers*, **Challengers were the largest group (~39%)**. In complex sales the share was even higher.
- **Relationship Builders were the least likely to be high performers (~7%).**
- The biggest driver of B2B loyalty was **the sales experience itself**: did the rep teach the buyer something new about their business? This mattered more than brand, product or price.

## The three Challenger skills
- **Teach**: bring a *commercial insight* that reframes how the buyer sees their own business.
- **Tailor**: adjust the message to each stakeholder's value drivers.
- **Take control**: be comfortable talking about money and pushing back. (Being assertive, not aggressive.)
