# Persuasion Science: Robert Cialdini, *Influence*

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Book (behavioral science) |
| **Author / Origin** | Robert Cialdini (Arizona State University) |
| **Year** | 1984; new and expanded edition 2021; *Pre-Suasion* 2016 |
| **Field** | Social psychology → sales, marketing, all persuasion |
| **Evidence tier** | **A: Data-backed** (peer-reviewed experiments plus years of field research *undercover in sales jobs*) |
| **Original** | *Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion*; [Influence at Work](https://www.influenceatwork.com/) |

## Why it made the cut
It's the scientific basis for *why* sales techniques work. Cialdini went undercover in car dealerships, fundraising and sales organizations, then tested what he saw in controlled experiments. Every framework in this library relies on one or more of these principles.

## The seven principles
| Principle | Meaning | Software sales application |
|---|---|---|
| **Reciprocity** | We repay what we receive | Give real value first: a free audit, benchmark data, a useful insight |
| **Commitment & Consistency** | We act in line with what we've said | Get small yeses: confirm pains in writing, mutual action plans |
| **Social Proof** | We follow people like us | "Teams like yours..." · logos and case studies *from the same segment* |
| **Authority** | We defer to experts | Expertise and insight (Challenger, Gap Selling), not titles |
| **Liking** | We say yes to people we like and who are like us | Genuine rapport, common ground, compliments |
| **Scarcity** | We want what's rare or disappearing | Real constraints only: implementation slots, pricing changes |
| **Unity** (added 2016) | We say yes to "one of us" | Shared identity: "fellow founders," same community |

## Key dialogue *(illustrative)*
- **Reciprocity:** "Before we talk about us, I put together a benchmark of how your onboarding time compares with 40 similar companies. Want me to walk you through it?"
- **Commitment:** "So we agree the goal is cutting close to 3 days by Q2. Shall I write that up so we're both working from the same plan?"
- **Social proof:** "Three of the five largest logistics SaaS companies use this exact workflow."
- **Scarcity (honest):** "Our implementation team has two slots in January. After that it's March."

## Watch-outs
- Fake scarcity and fake social proof destroy trust in B2B, where buyers talk to each other and check references.
- Cialdini himself stresses *ethical* use: point to principles that are actually present, rather than manufacturing them.
