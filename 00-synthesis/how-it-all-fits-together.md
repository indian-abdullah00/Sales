# How It All Fits Together

The sources in this library come from very different places: researchers watching tens of thousands of calls, an FBI hostage negotiator, startup founders, a Guinness-record car salesman, and companies that analyze hundreds of millions of recordings with AI. **They agree on far more than they disagree.** This document shows the overlap, the tensions, and how to combine them into a single approach for software sales.

---

## 1. The principles nearly every source agrees on

| # | Principle | Who says it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Diagnose before you prescribe.** Understand the problem before pitching. | Rackham (SPIN), Keenan (Gap), Sandler, Gong (11–14 questions), Cohan (demo), Dunford |
| 2 | **The buyer should talk more than you** (except on cold calls). | Gong (43/57), Sandler, Voss (tactical empathy) |
| 3 | **Quantify the cost of doing nothing.** | SPIN (Implication), Command of the Message (Negative Consequences), Gap (Impact), JOLT (status quo) |
| 4 | **Frame around a change in the world, not your product.** | Raskin/Zuora, Salesforce, Challenger (Reframe), Dunford (Insight), Sequoia ("Why now") |
| 5 | **Teach, don't tell.** Bring insight the buyer didn't have. | Challenger, Gap ("people buy from experts"), Pink (Clarity), Cialdini (Authority) |
| 6 | **Make it easy to say yes, and safe to say no.** | Sandler (UFC), Voss (no-oriented questions), Braun ("Is it a terrible idea...?"), JOLT (take risk off the table) |
| 7 | **Every interaction ends with a concrete next step.** | SPIN (Advances), Sandler (no mutual mystification), MEDDPICC (Decision Process) |
| 8 | **Proof beats claims.** | Zuora (step 5), Cialdini (Social Proof), YC (traction), Drift (we did it ourselves) |
| 9 | **Short and simple wins.** | Lavender/Hunter data, YC (one-sentence clarity), Jobs (one idea per slide) |
| 10 | **The biggest competitor is "no decision."** | JOLT (40–60% of losses), MEDDPICC (Competition includes doing nothing), Salesforce (the enemy is "Software") |

## 2. Where the sources disagree

| Tension | Side A | Side B | How to resolve it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Relationships vs. challenge** | Girard, Ziglar: relationships and liking win | Challenger: Relationship Builders were least likely to be top performers | *Both*: earn the right to challenge through expertise; relationships come **from** the value you add |
| **Ask questions vs. lead with insight** | SPIN, Gap: questions first | Challenger, Dunford: lead with a reframe | Open with a short insight (it earns attention), *then* go deep on questions |
| **Closing** | Ziglar, Belfort: always be closing | Rackham: classic closes hurt large deals | Transactional → close; complex → Advance |
| **Talk ratio** | Gong: listen 57% in discovery | Gong: talk *more* on successful cold calls | It depends on the stage: cold calls need relevance quickly, discovery needs listening |
| **Add value vs. limit information** | Classic selling: show more value | JOLT: more information makes indecision *worse* | Diagnose first: status quo → add value; indecision → recommend and limit |
| **Tactics vs. authenticity** | Belfort, Voss: specific techniques | Braun, Pink: detach from outcome, be honest | Techniques *amplify* real empathy; they can't replace it |

## 3. The unified software sales flow

Each stage uses the best-supported tools from across the library.

```
 PROSPECT ──▶ DISCOVER ──▶ PITCH / DEMO ──▶ QUALIFY & ADVANCE ──▶ NEGOTIATE ──▶ CLOSE ──▶ EXPAND
```

|---|---|---|---|

## 4. One example conversation that uses everything *(illustrative)*

> **[Cold call, Gong/30MPC]** "Hi Dana, it's Sam from Acme. This is a cold call. Can I have 30 seconds to tell you why I'm calling? ... I work with finance leaders at Series B SaaS companies who tell me month-end close is creeping past 10 days since they added a second billing system. Is that on your radar?"
>
> **[Up-Front Contract, Sandler]** "Thanks for taking the meeting. We have 30 minutes. I'll ask about how close works today, you'll ask about us, and at the end we decide together whether it's worth continuing. Fair?"
>
> **[Insight, Challenger/Dunford]** "Quick context: the average company your size went from 3 to 9 revenue data sources in two years. That's usually what breaks the close."
>
> **[Discovery, SPIN/Gap/Voss]** "Walk me through day one to day ten of your close... *Day ten?* ... What happens to the board deck when it slips? ... It sounds like there's pressure from the board on this."
>
> **[Critical event, SPICED]** "Is there a date when this has to be fixed? ... The Series C diligence in March, what happens if the numbers aren't clean by then?"
>
> **[Pitch, Zuora]** "Here's what we see: finance teams that win the next round close in three days..."
>
> **[Demo, Cohan]** "Let me start with the end: here's your board revenue report, reconciled as of this morning..."
>
> **[MEDDPICC]** "Who signs off on a purchase like this? Would you be open to presenting the business case to the CFO together?"
>
> **[Objection, Gong/Voss]** "It's too expensive." *(pause)* "Compared with what? ... You mentioned two analysts spend half their week on this..."
>
> **[JOLT]** "Honestly, for a team your size, I'd start with the Growth plan in one entity for 60 days, with an out if it doesn't cut close to 5 days."
>
> **[Collison install]** "If that works for you, let's connect your Stripe account now, while we're on the call."
